We are testing a new status page for NREC. It shows the state of our public
services, news and maintenance, and how the cloud is used.

News and maintenance notices can now be published from a file in the
[nrec-status repository](https://github.com/caleno/nrec-status) or from a
GitHub issue, in addition to our existing channels.
